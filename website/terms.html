Using Cashlytix

Cashlytix is a personal tracking tool. You enter your own figures, and the app calculates balances, totals and who owes whom.

Not financial advice

We do not give investment, tax or legal advice. Check important figures against your bank statements.

Your account and data

You own your data. Keep a backup, because data stored only on a device can be lost with the device. You can export or delete your data in the app.
