Using Cashlytix

Cashlytix is a personal tracking tool. You enter your own figures, and the app calculates balances, totals and who owes whom from them.

Not financial advice

Cashlytix does not give investment, tax or legal advice. Check important figures against your bank statements.

Your data

You own your data. Keep a backup, because data stored only on your device can be lost if the device is lost or the browser storage is cleared.
