What we collect

Without an account, nothing leaves your device. The transactions, accounts, budgets and friends you enter are stored locally in the app. If you sign in for sync, we store your email address and a private copy of your data so your devices match.

What we do not do

  • No ads or ad trackers
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No bank, SMS or contacts access

Where sync data lives

Sync data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, protected by per-user access rules.

Your choices

Export your data or delete everything, including the cloud copy, from the More tab.
