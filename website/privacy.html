Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Draft. This page describes how the app works today. It is not legal advice and will be reviewed before the Play Store release.

What we collect

Without an account, nothing leaves your device. The transactions, accounts, budgets and friends you enter are stored locally in the app (on the web, in your browser storage).

If you create an account for sync, we store your email address and a private copy of your Cashlytix data so your devices can match. Only you can read your copy.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no ad trackers
  • No analytics on your entries
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No bank, SMS or contact access

Where sync data lives

Sync data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, protected by per-user access rules.

Your choices

  • Export or back up your data from the More tab at any time.
  • Delete everything from the More tab. If you are signed in, this also removes the cloud copy.
  • To close your account, open an issue on GitHub.

Contact

Use GitHub Issues. A support email will be added before the Play Store release.
