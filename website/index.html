Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Cashlytix has no ads and no analytics. If you create an account to sync, your entries are also saved in your private cloud copy so your devices match.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself, which keeps your bank details with you. It is why logging has to be this fast.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan has limits on accounts, recurring entries, budgets and groups. Pro will lift them. Nothing is charged today.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup file from the More tab and restore it on the new phone, or sign in with sync turned on.

Can my friends see my entries?

No. Split bills are tracked in your own app. Shared groups where friends join with their own accounts are planned.

How do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub. We read every one.
