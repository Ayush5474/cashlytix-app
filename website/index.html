Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you create an account, a private copy is also saved so your phone and browser match. There are no ads and we do not sell data.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself, which keeps your bank details with you.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan has limits on accounts, recurring entries, budgets and groups. Pro will lift them.

Can I install it on my phone today?

Store listings are planned. Until then, download the Android file or add the web app to your iPhone home screen.
